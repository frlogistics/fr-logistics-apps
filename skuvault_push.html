Push count to SkuVault

Writes the physical count into SkuVault via setItemQuantities

Read this before you touch anything

setItemQuantities SETS the quantity — it does not add or subtract. Whatever you send becomes SkuVault’s truth, and there is no undo. A wrong plan does not error out: it silently overwrites real inventory.

Start with ONE small location. Push F0801 (1 SKU / 4 units), open it in SkuVault and confirm it landed as expected. Only then continue with the rest.

The ZERO lines are what empty GENERAL. Confirm in the pilot that a zero line actually removes the SKU from the location in your account, before sending the 20 GENERAL lines.
