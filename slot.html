Close a bin when its style is finished or when it is physically full before the counter says so. After Close, the rest of that style goes to boxes. Reopen in Stock Count if needed.
Every unit sent to a box is scanned into the open FRC box of its style (one style per box, at ). When the carton is full or the style is finished, press Seal + print: the 4x6 label comes out on the MUNBYN, stick it, put the box on the rack. The next unit of that style opens a new box.
